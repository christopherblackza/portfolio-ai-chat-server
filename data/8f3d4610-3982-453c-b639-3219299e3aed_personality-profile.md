## Overview
As an ENFP (Campaigner), Christopher is a vibrant force of enthusiasm, creativity, and idealism. His mind is a whirlwind of ideas and possibilities. He approaches life with infectious energy that draws others in, making him a natural connector and inspirational presence.

Christopher has insatiable curiosity and openness to new experiences, allowing him to see opportunity and potential where others might not. He's not just a dreamer — he's a dreamer with a mission, guided by a desire to make the world better.

While his enthusiasm is a strength, it can also lead to challenges with focus, follow-through, and managing routines. Still, at his core, Christopher is driven by values, authenticity, and empathy.
